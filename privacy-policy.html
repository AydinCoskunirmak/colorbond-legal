Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Meltz (the "App") collects, why, and the choices you have. It is published by Aydın Coşkunırmak ("we", "us", "our"), an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the App.

In short: Meltz does not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts, or location. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use an advertising identifier to personalize ads (see Section 1(e) and Section 3). The only other information sent off your device is an anonymous ID and the nickname/avatar/score you choose to show on the optional public leaderboard.

1.Information We Collect

a) Stored only on your device. Your game progress (current level), Gold balance, sound/vibration settings, and language preference are saved locally on your device using standard app storage. This information is never transmitted to us.

b) Anonymous identifier. When you first open the App, Google Firebase Authentication assigns your device a random, anonymous ID. This does not require and is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any other real-world identity.

c) Leaderboard information (optional feature). If you view or participate in the global leaderboard, the following is sent to and stored in our Google Cloud Firestore database, associated with your anonymous ID:

  • the nickname you choose to enter;
  • the emoji avatar you choose;
  • your highest level reached / score; and
  • the date and time this was last updated.

This information is publicly visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not enter your real name or any other personal information as your nickname.

d) Purchases (if enabled). If in-app purchases are available in your version of the App, they are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems. We do not receive, see, or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details — the app store provider handles this directly under its own privacy policy.

e) Advertising. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its ad-network partners may collect device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and general device/app information, and may use this information to show you more relevant ads and to measure ad performance. On iOS, this only happens if you grant permission when prompted (App Tracking Transparency); on Android, you can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device's settings. See Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" for details of Google's own practices.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, and we do not otherwise use analytics SDKs beyond the ad measurement described above.

2.How We Use Information

  • To operate the optional global leaderboard and display rankings;
  • To keep your local game progress, settings, and virtual currency balance on your device;
  • To show ads through Google AdMob, and (optionally, if you purchase it) to remove ads for your device;
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems reported to us directly by users.

We do not build our own behavioral or advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Ad personalization, where it happens, is performed by Google AdMob under its own privacy policy, not by us.

3.Third-Party Service Providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as our backend infrastructure provider to operate the leaderboard, and Google AdMob to display ads. These providers may process data on servers located outside of your country. Google acts as our data processor for these purposes; see Google's Privacy Policy, Firebase's data processing terms, and AdMob's data disclosure for details of Google's own practices.

4.Data Sharing

Other than the Firebase infrastructure described above, and disclosures required by law (for example, in response to a valid legal request), we do not share, sell, or rent your information to third parties.

5.Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Leaderboard data (nickname, avatar, score, anonymous ID) is retained until the periodic leaderboard reset described in the App, or until you request deletion as described in Section 7.

6.Children's Privacy

Meltz is a general-audience game with no age gate, and can be used by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as a real name, email address, home address, or phone number from any user, including children. The only information linked to a device is an anonymous ID together with a nickname and avatar that the player themselves chooses.

We ask parents and guardians to help children choose a nickname that does not contain personal information, and to supervise use of the leaderboard feature. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered personal information into the App, or you would like leaderboard data associated with your child's device deleted, please contact us using the details in Section 8 and we will act on your request.

7.Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), or U.S. state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because the App does not require an account, we identify your data using the anonymous ID stored on your device.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below. Where possible, please tell us your in-app nickname so we can locate the correct leaderboard entry. You can also simply change your nickname/avatar at any time from the Settings screen in the App, or reset your local progress by uninstalling the App.

8.International Data Transfers

Because we use Google Firebase, information associated with the leaderboard may be stored and processed on servers located in countries other than your own, including the United States, under Google's own safeguards for international data transfers.

9.Data Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure and access rules to protect leaderboard data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
